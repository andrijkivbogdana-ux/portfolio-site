About

Design, for me, has always been about the "why" behind the pixels.

I'm Bogdana — a Product Designer who works at the intersection of research, experimentation, and product thinking. I participate in A/B tests, dig into analytics, and use data to validate (or challenge) design decisions. For me, a shipped feature isn't done until I've seen how real users interact with it.

Over the years I've worked across mobile apps, email campaigns, and web products — building design systems, running usability research, and partnering with engineers to ship things that hold up in production.

Outside my main work, I co-founded Product Expedition — a program that helps designers grow from executors into product thinkers, by teaching them to connect their decisions to metrics and growth.

I'm also learning UI vibe coding — exploring how AI-assisted front-end tools are changing the way designers and developers collaborate.

I believe good design is invisible. But its results aren't.
